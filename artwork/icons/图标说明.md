# Hermland 图标

设计目标：让使用自托管 AI 的用户把 Hermland 记成随时连接自己 AI 助手的“轻盈信使”，用漂浮精灵的单一轮廓表达轻盈、联系和陪伴。

当前版是用户于 2026-10-11 按图片确认的“轻盈信使（漂浮精灵版）”：深靛蓝底、珍珠白头部、深色面部嵌面、两枚暖琥珀色眼睛与短飘带。保留原图的陶瓷体积、柔和阴影和透明边缘，没有重新生成、裁切或改变造型，只按比例导出标准尺寸。

原候选图曾以“雕塑 → 鸟 → 漂浮精灵”的顺序展示，但方案编号不同。此前按“3 号方案”安装了鸟形图，造成选择歧义。现在以本次确认的图片为准，不再用“第三个”作为图标身份。

## 文件与接入

- 当前母版：`hermland-icon-messenger-1024.png`，1024 × 1024 PNG，保留 macOS 圆角标体之外的透明区域。
- 原始选图：`exec-384d7994-a389-48d1-a98a-359e4a463d77.png`（原始尺寸 1254 × 1254；位于本次聊天的 generated_images 目录）。仓库内的当前母版可独立用于重建，不依赖该缓存目录。
- 旧版：`hermland-icon-bird-1024.png`（鸟形）、`hermland-icon-modern-1024.png`（扁平门翼）和 `hermland-icon-1024.png`（金色门翼），均保留，方便回退。鸟形 `.icns` 另存为 `hermland-icon-bird.icns`。
- App 资源：`../../macos/Resources/AppIcon.icns`，包含 16、32、128、256、512 点及对应 Retina 尺寸。
- `macos/Info.plist` 的 `CFBundleIconFile=AppIcon`；`macos/project.yml` 将该文件复制到 Bundle Resources。
- 已检查 64、32 和 16 像素轮廓，主要靠白色头部、深色面部与短飘带识别。16 像素下细节减少，但仍保留主要轮廓。

母版是 macOS 版本，不能直接当作无透明通道的 iOS App Store 图标使用。

## 生成方式与提示词

使用内置 ImageGen，`transparent_background=true`。原始生成图按比例规范化为 1024 × 1024；尺寸导出使用 app-icon-studio 的脚本，未手工重画或改色。

### 当前版：轻盈信使（漂浮精灵版）

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create one original macOS app-icon concept for Hermland, a personal remote client connecting the user to their own AI assistant. This is a NEW visual identity, independent of all earlier designs. Emotional idea: a quiet clever MESSENGER with presence and character, not an architectural container.
Output one square 1024x1024 PNG app-icon artwork, not a mockup or a contact sheet. An impeccable smooth dark midnight-indigo macOS rounded-square tile fills about 86% of the canvas. Genuine clean transparency ONLY outside this tile; no ragged edge or scraps. One large iconic subject, about 66% of tile, optically centered with generous breathing room. Believable softly sculpted three-dimensional form, restrained matte ceramic materials, controlled highlights and shadows, clean precise silhouette. Strong contrast at 32 and 16 pixels. Modern, thoughtful, adult, approachable, premium; not a toy-store cartoon or fantasy game badge.
Palette: midnight indigo, pearl ivory, and only a small warm amber accent. NO boxes, containers, cubes, slots, doors, gates, locks, horned doorway marks, lettering, branding text, watermark, screens, buttons, micro-circuitry, medical caduceus, floating particles, stars, cheap chrome, shiny gold or overpowering glow. Never copy an existing corporate/app logo or familiar film character.
Subject: an original compact floating AI-messenger sprite, expressed as a single sculptural pearl-ivory pebble-shaped head and a short flowing deep-ink collar/ribbon tucked underneath. An asymmetrical smooth dark face inset carries just two small warm-amber eyes with a calm alert expression, no mouth or smile. No ears, antennae, horns, helmet, arms or legs. The head has a distinctive softly tapered organic silhouette, slightly inclined, and the short collar adds a quiet sense of arriving with a message. Keep the face inset and eye placement unique, not an existing robot. Use slightly oblique view, subtle volume, confident proportions, a large visible light silhouette and quiet material contrast. Not spherical generic chatbot art. One memorable adult companion symbol with a restrained playful personality.
```

完整尺寸已保存在 `macos/Resources/AppIcon.icns`，构建不依赖维护者本机的图标工具或生成缓存。修改图标时可用 macOS 的 `sips` 导出标准尺寸，再用 `iconutil` 打包；保留原图比例与透明边缘。
